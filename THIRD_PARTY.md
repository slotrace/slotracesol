# Third-party software and fonts

- **GSAP 3.14.2 and ScrollTrigger 3.14.2** — GreenSock. Original copyright and license headers are preserved in `public/vendor/`. Terms: https://gsap.com/standard-license/ . Source: https://github.com/greensock/GSAP .
- **DM Sans and IBM Plex Mono** — loaded from Google Fonts, with local font fallbacks. See https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans and https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono .

No third-party API credentials are distributed with this repository.
